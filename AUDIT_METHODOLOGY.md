# Audit Methodology

A PivotControl AI audit is a structured communication performance analysis of a recorded enterprise sales call. It is not a transcript summary. It is not a coaching session. It is a scored, evidence-based assessment of how a seller maintained or lost authority across the full arc of a high-stakes conversation.

---

## Audit Structure

Every PivotControl AI audit contains eight sections:

### 1. Call Context & Objective
Documents the intake context before any scoring begins. Includes call type, defined objective, prior history, and speaker roles. This section determines which rubric applies and sets the frame for every finding that follows.

### 2. Executive Summary
Two to three paragraphs describing the call environment, the seller's role, and what the audit found at a high level. Ends with the PCI score and rating.

### 3. Metric Breakdown
Five metrics scored individually out of 20 each, with a specific observation for each metric. Observations are drawn directly from transcript evidence, not inferred or generalized.

### 4. Key Observations & Recommendations
A table of specific moments from the call, both gains and gaps, with what happened, what the recommendation is, and whether the finding is CONFIRMED, CONTEXT-REVISED, or NOT APPLICABLE.

### 5. Rapid-Fire Questioning Analysis (or Pivot Moments)
Identifies the specific windows on the call where authority was tested. For AD-led calls: the five pivot windows (open, discovery, VP interjection, SC window, close). For SC calls: the technical pressure windows. For AE calls: the rapid-fire questioning sequences.

### 6. Language Playback
Direct transcript quotes organized into two tables: Moments of Authority (with analysis of why each worked) and Moments to Rebuild (with a specific reframed version of each gap). Every reframed version is account-specific, not a generic template.

### 7. 30-Day Development Plan
Four-week action plan with a specific practice activity and a measurable success signal for each week. Focused only on confirmed findings, never on context-incorrect observations.

### 8. Final Assessment
Two-column table: Strengths to Protect and Development Areas. Followed by the Executive Readiness Trajectory, one to two sentences on where the seller is on the path to executive-level communication and what it would take to get there.

---

## Scoring Principles

**Evidence over inference.**  
Every scored observation must be anchored to a specific transcript moment, a timestamp, a direct quote, or a documented pattern across multiple exchanges. No finding is written without transcript evidence.

**Context determines the rubric.**  
The rubric applied must match the call type and the seller's role on that specific call. A finding that would be valid on a discovery call may be not applicable on an SC-led calibration session.

**Honest labeling.**  
Findings that don't survive context review are removed, not softened. The CONFIRMED / NOT APPLICABLE labeling system is the tool's commitment to accuracy over output volume.

**Refinements vs corrections.**  
For sellers scoring in the Capable or Executive range, development areas are described as refinements, specific, narrow adjustments to an already-functional foundation. The language of the audit reflects the actual gap, not a generic development framework.

---

## What Is Not Scored

- **Buyer behavior**, Buyers are not scored. Their questions, objections, and reactions are observed as context but never evaluated.
- **Technical accuracy**, Whether a seller's product claims were accurate is outside the PCI scope. The audit measures how claims were delivered, not whether they were correct.
- **Outcomes**, Whether a deal closed or a next step was confirmed is not a PCI input. Authority under pressure is measured at the communication level, not the outcome level.
- **Non-scoreable transcript segments**, Transcription artifacts, crosstalk, and segments with no clear speaker attribution are excluded from scoring.

## Audit Delivery Format

Client-facing audits are delivered as formatted `.docx` files using the PivotControl AI report template. The template includes:

- Cover page with client information
- All eight sections with consistent table formatting
- Color-coded scoring (green for strengths, yellow for context notes, blue for confirmed findings)
- Language Playback tables with direct transcript quotes and account-specific reframes
- 30-Day Development Plan with weekly success signals
