# Case Study: [AD] / the federal systems integrator

**Seller:** [AD], Strategic Account Director, the software vendor  
**Account:** a federal systems integrator  
**Call type:** SC-Led Executive Demo  
**Call duration:** ~85 minutes (two transcript segments)  
**Final PCI:** 88/100, Executive  

---

## Overview

This is the most complete audit in the PivotControl AI beta portfolio. It spans the full arc of a 12-month strategic account, from first contact in April 2025 to the culminating executive demo in 2026 with nine the federal systems integrator stakeholders.

The audit was also the most instructive for the product itself. The first version was scored without complete call context and produced a PCI of 54/100. [AD]'s feedback directly shaped the intake-first process that is now a permanent part of PivotControl AI methodology.

---

## The Call

A 85-minute executive demo with:
- 3 the software vendor participants (AD, SC, VP Sales)
- 5 the federal systems integrator stakeholders, 3 established, 3 new to the conversation
- A live FedRAMP authorization upgrade from the VP Sales mid-open
- A live note correction on non-production instance counts
- A full structured close with three named next steps and [Program Lead]'s confirmed commitment

[AD]'s role: relationship anchor, SC supervisor, closer. Not discovery lead, discovery was completed in the prior session.

---

## Key Findings

**Strengths confirmed:**
- Strongest opening architecture observed across all beta audits, structured 12-month recap, six findings confirmed by name, agenda locked before any content
- Live note correction turned into a credibility moment
- VP FedRAMP interjection handled with an immediate infrastructure + application layer probe
- Three-part close: new AP intelligence recap by stakeholder name, three next steps with named owners, [Program Lead]'s confirmed commitment

**Development areas:**
- Functional bridge lines for new AP stakeholders during the discovery recap
- Pre-call VP alignment to prevent uncoordinated interjections

**Resolved from prior audit:**
- Close ownership, fully confirmed in the second transcript segment

---

## Why This Case Study [Stakeholder]ers for the Product

The [AD] audit produced PivotControl AI's most important product insight:

> *"Grading a call without account context, prior conversation history, or the defined objective for that specific call will produce findings that are technically observed but strategically incorrect."*

This feedback directly produced:
1. The mandatory intake form process
2. The CONFIRMED / NOT APPLICABLE labeling system
3. The role-specific rubric for AD on SC-led calls
4. The account intelligence tier in the intake form

The audit that started as a 54/100 with context errors ended as an 88/100 with complete context, and generated the most important product improvement in the beta cycle.
