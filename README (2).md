# Case Study: [Founder] / the AI startup

**Seller:** [Founder], Founder, the AI startup  
**Account:** a prospective client (prospect)  
**Call type:** Informal Executive Meeting / Founder Peer Intro  
**Call duration:** ~45 minutes  
**Final PCI:** 84/100, Executive  

---

## Overview

This audit covers an informal Zoom meeting between [Founder] and a three-person group of established entrepreneurs: a seven-figure operator, a body armor company founder, and a former a major bank professional. The call included a live product demonstration of the SAM.gov contract monitoring dashboard built for a paying client (a paying client), along with real-time negotiation of scope and next steps for a potential new engagement with a prospective client.

---

## The Call

This was not a formal sales call. It was a peer evaluation, experienced entrepreneurs deciding whether the AI startup was worth their time and credibility to engage. The communication challenge was establishing founder-level authority with people who have seen a lot of pitches and have high pattern-recognition for who is real versus who is performing.

Key pressure moments:
- Host signaling time constraints mid-explanation
- Direct ChatGPT/Gemini differentiation question from the seven-figure entrepreneur
- Implicit credibility pressure from the age gap (28 in a room of established 40s-era operators)

---

## Key Findings

**Strengths confirmed:**
- Opened by framing the AI startup as a company with a mission, not a personal service or freelance offering. In a room of co-founders, this established peer positioning from sentence one.
- Live deployed proof, the SAM.gov dashboard was a real client deliverable, not a mockup. This is the strongest credibility signal available to an early-stage founder.
- Requirements-first close under pressure: *"Get me your requirements and I can have an MVP before the next meeting."* Process discipline signaling maturity to experienced operators.
- No defensive language throughout, even during the most challenging questions.

**Development areas:**
- ChatGPT/Gemini differentiation answer drifted tactical before recovering. Pre-load a 30-second account-specific reclaim phrase.
- Social cue detection, caught the host's time signal approximately one exchange late.
- Develop a signature reclaim phrase that ends the general-purpose tool comparison frame immediately and pivots to deployed proof.

---

## Why This Case Study [Stakeholder]ers for the Rubric

The [Founder] audit introduced a scoring criterion that doesn't appear in the AD or SC rubrics: **founder-level peer positioning**.

The decision to frame the AI startup as a company entity, not a personal skill set, is a communication choice made in the first sentence. In an informal peer setting with experienced entrepreneurs, that choice determines whether the room engages as a buyer evaluating a vendor or as a group of operators talking to one of their own.

This finding is being incorporated into the Founder/AE rubric as a specific criterion.
